About

Shopping, made easy.

Shop With OWELEGO is a personal shopper and errand service built on one promise: you send the list, we handle the rest — the walking, the comparing, the bargaining and the carrying.

Every list is shopped as if it were for our own home: carefully, honestly and at a fair price, whether you are in Lagos, elsewhere in Nigeria or abroad.

Vision

To build Shop With OWELEGO into a well-known, trusted brand, recognised for customer satisfaction and excellence, while expanding into more services.

Mission

To reduce shopping stress by helping clients plan, shop, pack and deliver their market needs with care, so every customer feels relaxed, valued and satisfied.

Questions

Good to know.

How do I place an order?

Send your list on WhatsApp or a DM on TikTok. We confirm the details, you make payment, and we go shopping.

When do I pay?

Payment confirms your booking. Once it is received, we head to the market and buy exactly what is on your list.

Is delivery included in the package price?

No. Delivery fees are charged separately and depend on your location. The service charge also does not include transport.

Which areas do you cover?

Any location and every market in Lagos, on the Island and the Mainland. We also deliver within and outside Nigeria.

I live abroad. Can you still shop for me?

Yes. We serve local and international clients. Send your list and tell us where it is going.

Which package should I choose?

Basic suits a few simple items from one market. Half-Day (up to 4 hours) suits a medium list, Full-Day (up to 7 hours) suits bulk or detailed lists, and 3-Day is for relocations, store restocking and large projects. If you're unsure, send your list and we'll advise.
